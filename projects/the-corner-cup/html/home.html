Welcome to The Corner Cup

Whether you're looking for your morning coffee, a homemade brunch, or somewhere to relax with friends, The Corner Cup offers expertly crafted drinks and freshly prepared food in a warm and welcoming environment. Every cup is made with care using quality ingredients, while our friendly team is dedicated to creating a relaxed atmosphere where everyone feels at home. Whether you're visiting for a quick takeaway or a leisurely catch-up, we're delighted to welcome you through our doors.

Our Story

Since opening in Stockton Heath, The Corner Cup has become a place where great coffee, quality food and friendly service come together. Inspired by a passion for bringing people together, we've created a space where the local community can enjoy expertly brewed coffee, fresh homemade dishes and delicious cakes. We believe every visit should be memorable, whether you're meeting friends, working remotely or simply taking a moment to unwind with your favourite drink.
